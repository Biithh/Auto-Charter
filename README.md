# Charter

Charter turns a song into a chart for YARG and Clone Hero. I built it with the help of Claude (AI), mainly because almost nobody charts Spanish music and charting by hand is really tedious.

**This is an early test version.** The charts have mistakes and I'm still improving it. If you try it, it helps a lot if you tell me which part of the song is wrong and what the problem is.

It makes charts in all difficulties for:

- Guitar
- Bass
- Drums
- Keys
- Pro keys
- Vocals (with synced lyrics)

It's trained on a lot of charts and it isn't perfect, but it works for casual players, or for charters who want a solid starting point to fix up. The program is in English and Spanish: choose the language in the top-right corner of the window.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11
- A powerful NVIDIA graphics card
- About 20 GB of free space (the libraries and AI models are downloaded during installation)

I've only tested it on this PC:

- Intel Core i9-14900KF processor
- NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 graphics card with 12 GB of VRAM
- 32 GB of DDR5 RAM

On this PC, a song of about 3 minutes takes about 17 minutes. I don't know the minimum it needs: with a graphics card with less memory it may be much slower or not work at all.

## Install and use

1. Download the code (green **Code** button → **Download ZIP**) and unzip it into a folder.
2. Double-click `Instalar Charter.bat`. The first time takes a while, because it downloads everything into that folder.
3. Double-click `Charter.bat`, drag your songs into the window, choose the output folder and press **Generar charts**.

More details (in Spanish) in [LEEME.txt](LEEME.txt).

## Rules: personal use only

**Charts made with Charter are only for you.** You can't publish, upload or share them anywhere, not even for free: no chart websites, Discord, Reddit, forums, links or song packs. Charter is meant to play songs nobody has charted, not to fill the places where people share hand-made charts. If you want a song charted well, there are charters who do it by hand.

Every chart it makes is marked as `Charter (IA)` in its `song.ini`, so sites and servers can spot and filter them.

Only use songs you have the right to use. Full terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

# Spanish

## Charter

Charter convierte una canción en un chart para YARG y Clone Hero. Lo he hecho con la ayuda de Claude (IA), sobre todo porque casi nadie chartea música en español y hacerlo a mano es muy tedioso.

**Es una primera versión de prueba.** Los charts tienen errores y lo sigo mejorando. Si lo pruebas, me ayuda mucho que me digas qué parte de la canción está mal y cuál es el problema.

Genera charts en todas las dificultades de:

- Guitarra
- Bajo
- Batería
- Keys
- Pro keys
- Voz (con la letra sincronizada)

Está entrenado con muchos charts y no es perfecto, pero sirve para jugadores casuales o para quien quiera crear charts y busque una base bastante buena desde la que empezar a corregir. El programa está en español y en inglés: el idioma se elige arriba a la derecha de la ventana.

- Windows 10 u 11
- Una gráfica NVIDIA potente
